Overview

Sound Native does not collect personal information. All pronunciation analysis, recordings, and progress data stay on your device. The app sends only anonymous usage statistics, described below.

Microphone

The app uses the microphone to analyze your pronunciation in real time. Audio is processed entirely on-device and is never recorded, uploaded, or transmitted.

Recordings

When you save a recording (shadowing, reading aloud, etc.), it is stored locally on your device only. Recordings are never sent to any server. You can delete them at any time from the Recordings Library inside the app.

Progress Data

Your practice history, accuracy scores, streaks, and exercise sessions are stored locally using standard iOS storage (UserDefaults and the app's Documents folder). Your practice history itself never leaves your device.

Usage Statistics

Sound Native collects anonymous usage statistics using TelemetryDeck to understand how the app's features are used and to improve them. This data contains no audio, recordings, or text you enter, is not linked to your identity, and is not used for tracking or advertising.

Advertising

Free users see Google AdMob ads. AdMob may use your advertising identifier (IDFA) to show relevant ads, subject to your tracking preference set in iOS Settings. Pro subscribers see no advertisements.

Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob — advertising (free tier only)
  • Apple StoreKit — in-app purchases
  • TelemetryDeck — anonymous usage statistics

Children's Privacy

Sound Native is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Data Deletion

Delete the app to remove all locally stored data. No personal data is held on external servers, and the anonymous usage statistics cannot be linked to you, so no further action is needed.
