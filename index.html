What is Sound Native?

Sound Native is an iOS app for serious English learners who want to understand why they sound foreign — and fix it systematically. It combines a real-time vowel analyzer, 25+ pronunciation drills, a guided lesson path, and anatomically-grounded mouth exercises.

Features

  • Real-time vowel analyzer — see exactly where your vowels land, compared with native targets
  • 25+ pronunciation drills: Vowel & Consonant Pairs, TH, American /r/, Stress, Intonation, Connected Speech, Shadowing, Schwa, Tongue Twisters, and more
  • Guided path — 28 short lessons in a sensible order, from foundations to fluent speech
  • Practice Any Word — look up any English word or phrase, see its sounds, hear it, and record yourself
  • Translations in more than 15 languages — see what a word means in your own language (Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Russian, Arabic, Hindi and more), or type or speak a word in your language to find out how to pronounce it in English. Runs on-device.
  • 30 physical mouth exercises with animated diagrams
  • Accent Diagnostic and ear-training quizzes to find your weakest sounds
  • Smart Queue — personalized practice based on your accuracy
  • Progress tracking with streaks, accuracy trends, a 30-day vowel map, and a 28-day calendar
  • Save and replay your own recordings
  • Native-language tips for Russian, Spanish, and Mandarin speakers

Frequently Asked Questions

Which voice range should I choose?

Go to Settings → Your Voice Range and tap Detect From My Voice: read one short sentence and the app suggests a range. You can also pick one yourself: Lower (deep voice), Below Middle (typical male voice), Middle (between typical male and female), Above Middle (typical female voice) or Higher (light or child-like voice). The range sets the native-speaker targets you're compared against in the Analyzer, Challenge, Minimal Pairs and Vowel Map.

A vowel I'm sure I said correctly keeps showing as a neighboring vowel. Why?

Every voice resonates a little differently, so first check your voice range. If the readings are consistently off in the same direction, try the range one step up or down and see which one matches your voice best.

The Analyzer isn't picking up my voice. What can I do?

Practice in a quiet room and speak at a normal volume, a little closer to the phone. In Sustain mode, hold one vowel steady; in Word mode, say one word at a time with a short pause between words. If background noise is triggering readings when you aren't speaking, raise Settings → Microphone → Mic Sensitivity; if quiet speech isn't picked up, lower it.

What's the difference between Sustain and Word mode?

Tap the button above the vowel chart to switch. Sustain is for holding one vowel steady ("eeee") so you can watch it settle on the chart. Word is for saying a real word, like "bit" or "boot", and the app detects the vowel inside it, which is closer to how you actually speak.

Why does the app need microphone access?

To hear and analyze your pronunciation. All audio is processed on your iPhone and never uploaded. If you turned access off, go to iPhone Settings → Sound Native → Microphone.

Which accent does Sound Native teach?

American English.

How do I get tips for my native language?

Go to Settings → My Native Language. Tips are available for Russian, Spanish and Mandarin speakers; choose Other for general guidance.

Can I see translations in my own language?

Yes. Go to Settings → Translate Dictionary Words and pick your language from more than 15, including Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Russian, Arabic, Hindi, Vietnamese, Turkish, Polish and Ukrainian. Your iPhone will offer to download that language pack (an English pack may be needed too). After that, Practice Any Word or Phrase shows each word's translation, and you can type or speak a word in your own language to find out how to pronounce it in English. Translation runs on your iPhone and needs iOS 18 or later. If you see "Translation unavailable", the language pack hasn't finished downloading yet: wait a moment and search again. Direct translations of single words can occasionally pick a less common meaning.

What's free, and what's in Pro?

Free: the Vowel Analyzer, all 30 mouth exercises, the Accent Diagnostic and listening tests, the Phoneme Chart, Challenge and Smart Queue, plus one Pro drill free every day (marked with 🎁). Pro unlocks all 25+ drills and removes ads, as a monthly, yearly or one-time purchase.

How do I restore my Pro purchase?

Go to Settings → Upgrade to Pro and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID you bought it with.

Are my recordings stored online?

No. Your recordings and progress stay on your iPhone. The app sends only anonymous usage statistics. See the privacy policy.

How do I restart the guided lessons from the beginning?

Go to Settings → Reset Guided Syllabus Progress. This only resets which lessons are marked done; your accuracy stats, streak and recordings are kept.

Privacy

All audio is processed on-device. Your voice recordings and practice history are stored locally and never transmitted; the app sends only anonymous usage statistics. See our full privacy policy.
